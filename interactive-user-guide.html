Administrator Operations & Management Guide

Managing daily timesheets, overtime adjudication, payroll generation, user approvals, and audit trails.

Timesheet Approvals • DTR Filings

Reviewing & Bulk Approving Filings

In DTR Filings & Approvals, review entries with accomplishments. Use the Bulk Approve button in the header to approve all clean entries (entries without overtime) in a single click.

Conflict of Interest Rule: Administrators cannot approve their own DTR records. Another admin or super admin must review administrative punches.
Overtime Decisions • Extra Hours Queue

Adjudicating Extra Hours

Sessions exceeding daily or weekly limits route to the Extra Hours Queue. Admins can choose:

  • Approve All: Pays both regular and extra hours.
  • Approve Regular Only: Pays regular portion; excess extra hours are declined with a mandatory note sent to the employee.
  • Reject / Decline Session: Rejects the entire session (0.00h payable) with a mandatory justification note (e.g. erroneous filing).
Payroll Batches • Pay Periods & Payroll

Pre-Close Checklist & Closing Pay Periods

Before closing a pay cycle, click Run Pre-Close Check. The system verifies there are 0 unreviewed filings, 0 pending extra hours, and 0 open sessions. Once clean, click Close Period and Lock Period.

Prevents Retroactive Modifications
Staff Onboarding • Employees & Wages

Approving New Registrations & Setting Wages

Newly registered staff land in the Pending Registrations queue. Admins assign their role, department, clock-in permission, and starting hourly wage before activation.

Admin Wage Isolation: Regular administrators cannot view or modify their own hourly pay rate or the pay of other administrators. Only the System Administrator can do so.

Searchable Troubleshooting & FAQ

Instant answers to the most common questions and issues across all user roles.

Q1: I tried to clock in or file DTR, but the system says "No hourly wage has been configured".

Cause: To guarantee payroll integrity, the platform requires an active hourly rate before time entries can be filed.
Resolution: An administrator must go to Employees & Wages, click Set Wage on your profile, specify your hourly rate, and enter the effective date.

Q2: Why does my manual DTR entry say "Pay period covering date is CLOSED or LOCKED"?

Cause: Management has finalized that pay cycle. Standard users cannot submit retroactive entries into closed periods.
Resolution: File a Correction Request, or contact the System Administrator to evaluate whether the period should be temporarily unlocked.

Q3: An employee forgot to clock out and left for the day. How do we fix this?

Resolution: An administrator should navigate to DTR Filings & Approvals, filter status by Open Sessions, click Close Session on Behalf, specify their actual departure time, input an audit reason, and submit.

Q4: How do employees know if their overtime / extra hours were declined?

Resolution: On the employee's My DTR Records tab, the entry displays the Approved or Adjusted status badge. Clicking the entry reveals the manager's review note explaining why excess hours were declined.

Q5: Can regular administrators see how much other administrators earn?

Resolution: No. All administrator wages are strictly hidden from regular administrators. Only the root System Administrator can view and configure administrator compensation.

Q6: What happens if an employee works overnight across midnight?

Resolution: When Time Out is numerically earlier than Time In (e.g. 22:00 to 06:00), the system automatically adds 24 hours (1440 minutes) to calculate the full 8.00h shift cleanly without error or manual date splitting.
